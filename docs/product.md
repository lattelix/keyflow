# Product brief

## Promise
Choose music you genuinely want to play. Keyflow determines what you need to learn, teaches those prerequisites in context, breaks the piece into achievable practice units, checks practice where possible, and gradually removes assistance until you can perform independently.

## Core loop
Choose piece → assess prerequisites → learn missing skill → drill transition/pattern → practice phrase by hand → combine hands → join sections → perform → review weak spots.

## Product areas
Home, Songs, Learn, Library, Practice, Progress, Settings.

## Reference journey
Another Love is a UX reference only. Development fixtures must use public-domain/original/licensed material.

## MVP success
A new learner can import/open a legal MusicXML/MIDI piece, understand what to do next, practice a selected passage at reduced tempo with loop and hand controls, see keyboard/note guidance, save progress locally, and resume without an account.
